About Student Learning Institute

Student Learning Institute (SLI) is an academic coaching institute in Forbesganj, Bihar, serving school students and senior-secondary students. Our aim is to help every student build strong understanding through guided learning, practice and revision.

  • Classes 1–10, all subjects
  • Classes 11–12 in Arts and Science
  • Academic practice and revision
  • Monthly progress reporting
  • Additional support for students who need it
